How the scan works: for the trade type you pick, it tests every side (for Over / Under that is Over 0–8 and Under 1–9) against every entry on the first 70% of ticks, keeps only entries that also beat chance on the last 30% (which they were not picked from), then ranks them by how unlikely the combined result is by luck. Edge is the last-30% hit rate minus the chance of that streak with no entry. Score is that unlikeliness (higher is rarer). A signal is labelled strong or possible only if its score beats what the best of all the combinations tested would reach by luck alone. In 80 test scans on simulated random data with nothing real in it, "strong" still showed up about 5% of the time and "possible" about 11% of the time, and on simulated data with a real pattern planted the scan found the right market and entry most of the time at 3000–5000 ticks. Deriv synthetic digits are generated randomly, so expect "likely luck" most of the time, and treat 2 and 3 digit entries with extra caution because they rest on very few samples.
